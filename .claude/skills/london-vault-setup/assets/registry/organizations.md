# Registry — Organizations

Guilds, livery companies, religious houses, trading companies, civic institutions.

## Naming convention

- **Livery companies**: the formal name — `Worshipful Company of Mercers` — with the short form
  (`Mercers' Company`, `the Mercers`) in `aliases`.
- **Religious houses**: dedication plus type — `Holy Trinity Priory, Aldgate`, `St Bartholomew's
  Priory`. A house and its church are one note unless the church outlived the house (as at St
  Bartholomew-the-Great), in which case they are two, linked `succeeded-by`.
- **Civic bodies**: `Corporation of London`, `Court of Aldermen`, `Court of Common Council`. These
  are organizations; the **idea** of civic liberty is a `concept` note. Both can exist and link.
- **Successor bodies**: a renamed or refounded body follows the name-drift rule (§3) — same
  continuous institution, one note plus aliases; genuinely new body on the old footing, two notes
  linked `succeeded-by`.
