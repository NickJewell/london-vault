# <% Concept %>

What the term means, as the source uses it. Concepts are terms and institutions-as-ideas —
"sanctuary", "the liberty", "the freedom of the City", "ward-mote". If it is a body of people it
is an `organization`; if it is an idea about how a body works, it is a concept, and the two link.

Watch for concepts whose meaning shifts across periods — "liberty" as a legal exemption is not
"liberty" as a political ideal. Where the source distinguishes them, so should the vault: one note
per meaning, linked, rather than one note that quietly means both.
